THE WONDERS

Beneath the Ocean

When satellite radar captures an oil slick at 04:30 UTC, ocean currents and winds have already moved the hydrocarbon mass 20 to 40 kilometers from where it was secretly dumped. Conventional systems commit the fatal mistake of accusing whichever ship happens to be closest right now.

NAUTRACE solves this evidentiary crisis using physical Lagrangian backward hindcasting. By coupling hourly Copernicus ocean currents, Stokes wave drift, and tides, we rewind time hour by hour to pinpoint the true culprit ship with mathematical certainty.
