Samundar Ka CID & Forensic Detective — Poore Project Ko Aasan Bhasha Me Samjho

Yeh document khas taur par hamari poori team ke liye banaya gaya hai taaki har ek member (bina kisi marine ya complex physics background ke bhi) project ke har pehlu ko dil se samajh sake aur judges ke saamne confident hokar bol sake.

1

Pehle Ek Real-Life Kahani Samjho (Samundar Ka Hit-and-Run)

🌊 Nadi Aur Pul Ki Kahani:

Maan lijiye ek behne wali tez nadi (river) hai.
Ek aadmi pul (bridge) par khada hota hai aur nadi me ek tel ka dabba fenk kar wahan se bhaag jata hai.

3 ghante baad police wahan aati hai aur dekhti hai ki pul ke 10 kilometer aage nadi me tel beh raha hai.
Aur theek us tel ke bagal me ek innocent naav wala machhli pakad raha hai.

Agar police us machhli pakadne wale ko pakad kar jail me daal de ki "Tel ke paas to tum hi khade the, tumne hi fenka hai!" — to yeh sarasar galat aur be-insaafi hogi! Kyunki tel to 3 ghante pehle 10 kilometer peeche pul se fenka gaya tha!
🛑 Yahi Sabse Badi Problem Hoti Hai Samundar Me:

Bade-bade cargo ships aur crude oil tankers apna engine ka ganda oily kachra (bilge waste) samundar me chupke se dump kar dete hain port cleaning ke laakhon rupaye bachane ke liye. Jab satellite space se photo leta hai, tab tak hawa aur leharon ke bahav se wo tel 30 se 50 kilometer aage beh chuka hota hai.

🔍 Nautrace Ka Asli Kaam:
Nautrace samundar ka ek Digital Forensic Detective (CID / CBI) hai. Yeh satellite ki photo dekh kar time ko rewind (ulta) chalata hai, pata lagata hai ki tel kahan se behna shuru hua tha, aur us time par wahan se kaunsa jahaaz guzar raha tha.

Guide Page Ke 6 Tabs Ka Poora Khulasa

Tab 1: The Problem We Solve (Asli Dikkat Kya Hai?)
❌ Baaki Log Kya Galti Karte Hain:
College aur hackathons me aam taur par teams bas ek AI banati hain jo photo me tel pehchanta hai, aur uske theek bagal wale jahaaz ko mujrim bol deta hai. Isse be-kasoor ships par jhootha case ban jata hai.
✅ Nautrace Kya Alag Karta Hai:
Hum physics aur leharon ke bahav ke hisaab se tel ko ulta chala kar dekhte hain (Hindcasting) ki 2 se 6 ghante pehle wo tel kahan tha. Isse true polluter pakda jata hai.
Tab 2: End-to-End Pipeline (Aasman Se Courtroom Tak Ka Safar)

Hamara pura system 8 aasan steps me kaam karta hai:

1
Sentinel-1 Satellite Photo (Radar Camera)
Yeh aisi radar satellite hai jo badal (clouds) aur raat ke ghan-ghor andhere me bhi samundar ki saaf photo le sakti hai.
2
Asli Tel Hai Ya Dhokha? (Look-Alike Filter)
Har kaala dhabba tel nahi hota! Hawa band hone par samundar ka paani mirror ki tarah shant ho jata hai aur kaala dikhta hai. System pehle hawa check karta hai ki kya sach me tel hai ya sirf shant paani.
3
Hawa Aur Leharon Ki Raftaar (Metocean Data)
Europe ke mausam aur ocean models se data milta hai ki paani aur hawa kis disha me beh rahe the.
4
Tel Ko Ulta Chalana (Hindcast Simulation)
Hum 500 chote-chote tel ke kankaron ko computer me ulta daudate hain ki wo kahan se nikal kar aaye the.
5
Crime Zone Ka Anda-aakar Ghera (Origin Ellipse)
Jahan se tel nikla, wahan hum 50% core zone (Yellow) aur 90% outer search zone (Cyan) ka ghera banate hain.
6
Jahaazon Ki GPS List (AIS Trajectory)
Samundar ke sabhi jahaazon ke GPS data ko clean karte hain aur dekhte hain ki us time ghere ke andar kaun tha.
7
Kaunsa Jahaaz Mujrim Hai? (Scoring Engine)
Maths calculate karta hai ki kis jahaaz ka track aur time match hua. Agar sab door the, to Unknown source jeet jata hai.
8
Court-Ready Forensic Report (Convex AI)
Judge ke saamne pesh karne ke liye SHA-256 digital signature aur proof ke saath formal report tayyar hoti hai.
Tab 3: Mathematics & Physics (Bina Darr Ke Samjho)

UI me jo mathematical equations dikhte hain, unka aasan matlab yeh hai:

1. Particle Motion Equation (dx/dt)
Tel ki kul raftaar = (Samundar ka paani) + (Leharon ka dhakka) + (Hawa ka 3% dhakka). Tel surface par sail (pal) ki tarah hawa se dhakelta hai.
2. Mahalanobis Ellipse (Ghera Anda-aakar Kyun?)
Circle kyun nahi? Kyunki hawa aur lehar ek specific disha me beh rahi hoti hain. Isliye shaq ka daayra gol nahi, balki leharon ki disha me lamba (oval) banta hai.
🎛️ Interactive Simulator Ka Khel:
Aap guide page par slider hila kar khud dekh sakte hain:
• Agar jahaaz 1.3 km paas tha: Score 0.79 (Top Suspect!).
• Agar jahaaz 10 km door tha: Score gir kar 0.15 ho jayega aur system bolega: "Yeh jahaaz innocent hai, shaq Unknown Vessel par hai!"
Tab 4: 7 Explainability Scores (CBI Ki 7 Kasam)

Jab judge poochta hai ki "Aapne is Tanker ko hi suspect kyun mana?", to Nautrace yeh 7 sub-scores dikhata hai:

Score Name Asli Matlab Tanker Ka Score
1. Distance (Spatial) Kya jahaaz us point ke kitna paas se guzra? 92/100 (Sirf 1.3 km door)
2. Time (Temporal) Kya usi ghante me guzra jab tel dump hua tha? 86/100 (Sirf 11 min ka farq)
3. Heading (Disha) Kya jahaaz usi disha me ja raha tha jis line me tel baha? 73/100 (Almost parallel)
4. Envelope Overlap Kya jahaaz ne 50% ya 90% shaq wale ghere ko cross kiya? 90/100 (Ghere ke beech se nikla)
5. GPS Quality Kya jahaaz ka GPS continuous tha ya beech me band hua? 98/100 (Bilkul continuous)
6. Speed Change Kya achanak jahaaz slow hua tha? (Dump karte waqt speed ghatate hain) 40/100 (Thoda speed drop tha)
7. Stability Hawa-paani 15% hilane par bhi kya yahi jahaaz #1 rehta hai? 88/100 (Very stable)
3

Real-World Issues & Hard Truths (Asliyat Me Kahan Faste Hain?)

Yeh hamare project ka sabse honest hissa hai. Judges ko honesty bahut pasand aati hai:

1. Hawa Kam Hona (< 3 m/s)
Jab hawa band hoti hai, to paani shant hokar kaala dikhta hai. System weather data se check karta hai, agar hawa 3 m/s se kam ho to pehle hi warn kar deta hai ki "Dhokha ho sakta hai!".
2. Exact Time Kisi Ko Nahi Pata
Satellite to bas photo leti hai. Tel 2 ghante pehle gira ya 6 ghante pehle, koi nahi jaanta. Isliye hum ek single line nahi banate, balki 2 se 6 ghante ka pura time-window check karte hain.
🚨 Masterstroke: Chalu Captains Aur GPS Band Karna (Dark Vessels):
Kayi baar tel dump karne wale jahaaz apna GPS (AIS) 15–20 minute ke liye switch off kar dete hain.
Agar koi registered ship match nahi hota, to Nautrace be-kasoor par ilzaam nahi lagata! Wo bolta hai: "UNKNOWN / Non-AIS Source (0.86)".
Yeh international maritime law (IMO Resolution A.1106) ka niyam hai.

5-Minute SIH Jury Playbook (Judges Ke Saamne Kya Bolna Hai?)

Jab SIH ke judges screen par Nautrace dekhenge, to hamari team ko yeh 5 steps bolne hain:

Minute 1: Trap Samjhao (Problem Statement)
"Respected Jury: Baaki log satellite photo me tel dekh kar uske sabse kareeb wale jahaaz ko mujrim bol dete hain. Par samundar me tel hawa aur leharon se ghanton me 40 km beh jata hai. Isliye paas wala jahaaz innocent hota hai. Humne ek real forensic pipeline banayi hai."
Minute 2: Physics Hindcasting Dikhao
"Screen par dekhiye: Humne 500 tel ke kankaron ko RK4 physics aur Copernicus ocean currents ke zariye ulta rewind kiya hai, jisse 50% aur 90% shaq ka ghera nikalta hai."
Minute 3: Timeline Scrubber Chalao
"Jaise hi hum scrubber ko 02:50 UTC par le jaate hain, aap dekhenge ki Container Ship to abhi paas aaya hai, par Tanker 2 ghante pehle theek usi ghere se guzra tha jahan se tel nikla tha."
Minute 4: Unknown Hypothesis Ka Jadoo
"Humne IMO Resolution A.1106 follow kiya hai. Agar jahaaz ne GPS band kiya tha, to humara model kisi innocent par ilzaam nahi lagata, Unknown source ko rank #1 deta hai."
Minute 5: Court Dossier Export Karo
"Hum [Generate AI Brief] dabate hain. Convex AI court me pesh karne layak formal English report banata hai, jisme SHA-256 digital signatures lagte hain jo courtroom me legally valid hain."
